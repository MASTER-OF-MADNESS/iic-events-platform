Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2023

The Institution's Innovation Council (IIC) at VIT respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when registering for events, such as your name, email address (via Google OAuth), and student registration number.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to manage event registrations, generate certificates, and communicate with you about upcoming innovation activities.

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access or disclosure.
